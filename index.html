What we do

Three divisions, one team on site and in the cloud.

PLC · SCADA · DCS · Servo & drive systems

Industrial Automation

We design, build and maintain the control systems that run the factory floor, from a single panel to a full plant-wide automation rollout, backed by on-site engineers and maintenance contracts.

Design & engineering

  • PLC programming and panel design (Siemens, Allen Bradley, Mitsubishi, Delta)
  • SCADA system development and HMI configuration
  • DCS implementation for process industries
  • Servo motor and AC drive commissioning
  • Control panel design, fabrication and testing

Installation & support

  • Building electrical wiring and automation installation projects
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for automation systems
  • On-site and on-call engineer deployment for factory maintenance
  • Commissioning, testing and operator handover

Industrial IoT · Cloud analytics & reporting

IoT & Data Platform

We turn machine and factory data into decisions: collecting it at the source, moving it to the cloud, and putting it in front of the right people at the right time.

Data & connectivity

  • Data acquisition from PLC, sensors and field devices
  • Secure cloud data pipelines (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
  • Production data integration with ERP and SAP systems
  • Custom cloud data fetch and visualization software

Insight & reporting

  • Real-time monitoring dashboards for production and energy
  • Automated report generation and delivery via Email and SMS
  • Predictive maintenance using historical machine data
  • OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) tracking platforms

About us

Infornex Systems is a multi-domain technology company headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. We deliver end-to-end solutions across Industrial Automation, IoT & Cloud Data Platforms and Energy Infrastructure, all under one roof.

Our unique strength is the ability to serve a single client across all three divisions: from installing a PLC on the factory floor, to connecting it to the cloud, and on to smart energy infrastructure.
